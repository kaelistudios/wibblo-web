Tutorials

Step by step, with screenshots of the app.

How to make a bingo

See the steps with screenshots

  1. Step 1. Choose what fills the cards (words, numbers, images or questions), their size and how many you want.

  2. Step 2. Add the content: type your words or pick images from the image bank. You can also use your own photos.

  3. Step 3. Give it a title and, if you like, a background color or image.

  4. Step 4. Tap Generate, check the preview and export the cards to PDF.

How to make a word search

See the steps with screenshots

  1. Step 1. Type the words to find or tap “Use ready-made content”. You can group them under a question or category.

  2. Step 2. Choose the grid size and whether words can go diagonally or backwards.

  3. Step 3. Give it a title and a color, and decide whether to include the answer key.

  4. Step 4. Tap Generate, check the preview and export to PDF.

How to make a crossword

See the steps with screenshots

  1. Step 1. Type each word with its clue, or tap “Use ready-made content”.

  2. Step 2. Give it a title and a color, and decide whether to include the answer key.

  3. Step 3. Tap Generate: Wibblo crosses the words for you. Check the preview and export to PDF.

How to make a math worksheet

See the steps with screenshots

  1. Step 1. Choose the operations, the number range and the rules, for example no negative results.

  2. Step 2. Choose the layout (inline or vertical), the number of exercises and of different versions.

  3. Step 3. Tap Generate, check the preview and its answer key, and export to PDF.

How to make a secret code

See the steps with screenshots

  1. Step 1. Choose the type of code: symbols, numbers or math operations.

  2. Step 2. Type the phrases to encode or tap “Use ready-made content” (sayings, for example).

  3. Step 3. Give it a title and choose how many different codes you want.

  4. Step 4. Tap Generate, check the preview and export to PDF.

Frequently asked questions

How do I print double-sided?

Some worksheets have a back side: bingo cards with a decorative back, double-sided flashcards and pairs or domino cards with backs. Turn on double-sided printing on your printer. For portrait pages, flip on the long edge; for landscape pages (bingo with 2 cards per page), flip on the short edge. If your printer can't print double-sided, print the odd pages first, put the paper back in and then print the even pages.

What is the QR code at the bottom of the worksheets?

It leads to this page, so anyone who gets a worksheet knows how it was made and can download Wibblo. With Wibblo Pro you can hide the footer in Settings.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone or iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Wibblo Pro. On Android: Google Play → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. You can also get there from the app, on the Wibblo Pro screen → “Manage subscription”. Pro stays active until the end of the period you already paid for.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the Wibblo Pro screen and tap “Restore purchases”, using the same Apple or Google account you bought with. The app also checks automatically every time you open it.

Which languages is Wibblo in?

Spanish, English, French and Portuguese. The first time it uses your phone's language, and you can change it anytime in Settings. Ready-made content is available in each language too.

Do I need an account or internet?

No. Wibblo doesn't ask for an account and works offline, except to buy or restore Pro. Your worksheets are saved only on your phone.
